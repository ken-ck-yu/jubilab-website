From lab samples to result.

Scientists order. Wet labs run the samples. Today the two sides are joined only by email and Excel. Jubilab joins them with one order, one place to follow it and one clean result.

  • If you send samples out

    For research labs and spin-outs. One order form for every outside lab, one place to follow it, and clean results with your own sample names.

  • If you run a lab or core

    Your clients still order from you. We aim for fewer wrong order sheets, and you update the status with a click instead of an email. You keep your own tools and your own relationship with your clients.

Order once. Follow every step.

This is what we are building. None of it is released yet.

  1. Step 1

    Order once

    Describe your samples and what you want, one time. Jubilab passes it to the lab in the form that lab needs.

  2. Step 2

    Follow every step

    See when the sample is received, whether it passed QC, when the run starts and when results are ready. No more emails to ask.

  3. Step 3

    Get clean results back

    Results come back with your own sample names. A failed sample shows up as failed, not as missing.

Every fact shows who told us: the lab’s own system, an email we read, or nobody yet. We never fill a gap with a guess.

Jubilab is not a marketplace and not an ELN: it sits between your lab and the labs you order from. It does not set prices, control instruments or handle human clinical diagnostics.

What week one looks like, as we plan it: you send us the order sheet you already use, with no new login and no lab software needed to start. We turn it into one order. The lab updates the status in a way that suits them. You get one clean result back.

Nothing changes on your side on day one.

For labs, cores and boutique providers, and for the teams that use them. You join in the way that suits you, and you can stop at any step. Early access is by invitation, and none of this is released yet.

  • Your existing tools

    We read the order emails and courier tracking you already have, and take updates from your lab software. Facts we read from emails are marked as less certain. Facts that come from your lab software are marked as confirmed.

  • A Jubilab status page

    A status page with four buttons (received, QC, run started, results ready) and drag-and-drop result upload. The status page is less work than writing the email.

  • Your instruments

    For labs we work with or own: run data read live from your sequencers. We read it. We do not control the instrument.

We work with your existing tools without disruption and let AI assistants place and follow orders.

You keep your order process, your lab software and your instruments. Take orders from AI assistants and institutional buyers without changing how you work.

What a lab gets, as we plan it: we aim for fewer wrong order sheets, fewer “where is my sample?” emails, orders that arrive in one tidy form, and no change to how you work.

Why now: every AI agent stops at the lab door.

AI agents can already draft a sequencing plan and reason about a result file. They stop at the lab, because a lab cannot be ordered from by software: in the providers we opened, we found no way to do it. We are building the door.

In 2025 Benchling began direct ordering for a few suppliers and left sequencing and assays out. Benchling is a name of its owner. Jubilab is not affiliated with it.

At Biligene, our own sequencing orders lived in forty email threads. That is why we are building this, and why Biligene’s own sequencing orders are planned to run through it first.

Genetics sequencing first, then any lab service.
