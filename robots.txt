# Launch rules for jubilab.io. Source: robots.launch.txt, copied over robots.txt by scripts/go-public.py (docs/launch-checklist.md step 1).

# AI crawler policy, decided by the owner on 2026-10-16:
#   ALLOW search and answer bots (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User)
#   and ordinary search crawlers. They fetch pages so that an assistant or search engine can show and cite
#   them to a person who asked.
#   BLOCK the training bots GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and CCBot.
# Robots rules are requests, not enforcement: well-behaved crawlers honour them, others may not.
# Names are the published user-agent tokens; check each vendor's documentation before changing them.
#   Training bots (blocked below):
#     GPTBot           OpenAI model training
#     ClaudeBot        Anthropic model training
#     Google-Extended  controls use of pages for Google's Gemini training and grounding (does not affect Google Search ranking)
#     CCBot            Common Crawl, whose dataset many models train on
# Every named group is separate: a crawler uses the most specific group that names it, so the groups
# below do not affect anyone else, and "User-agent: *" is the fallback for all other crawlers.

User-agent: GPTBot
User-agent: ClaudeBot
User-agent: Google-Extended
User-agent: CCBot
Disallow: /

User-agent: *
Allow: /
