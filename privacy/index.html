Privacy

This page describes what the Jubilab website does with information about you. The short version: it counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (always, without cookies) and, only if you accept, Google Analytics, and uses what you send through the early-access form only for early access.

Browsing this site

The site is static pages served from Cloudflare Workers. It loads fonts and images from the site itself, not from other companies. The only third-party scripts are the two analytics services described below. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and is always on. Google Analytics does use cookies (_ga and _ga_<container id>, described below) and runs only after you accept it in the cookie notice. Apart from those Google cookies, the only things the site stores in your browser are your light or dark theme choice (local storage key jubilab-theme) and your answer to the cookie notice (local storage key jubilab-consent, described below), which never leave your device. Like any web host, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) receives your IP address and basic request details when your browser fetches a page, and handles them under its own privacy statement.

We use Google Analytics, a service from Google, to count visits and see how the site is used, but only if you accept it. The first time you visit, a cookie notice (“Cookies on jubilab.io”) asks you to choose Accept analytics cookies or Reject analytics cookies. Until you accept, no request is sent to Google and no Google cookie is set. If you choose Reject analytics cookies, nothing is loaded from Google. If you choose Accept analytics cookies, a script from Google is loaded and collects page views, your approximate location (worked out from your IP address; Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses), your device and browser type, the page that referred you, and how you move through our pages. It sets cookies in your browser named _ga and _ga_<container id> to recognise a returning browser. This data is sent to Google and may be processed outside the UK and the EU. Google keeps it for a limited period set in our account. The early-access form is separate: what you type into it goes only to our own server and is not sent to Google. Your answer is stored in this browser only (local storage, key jubilab-consent, with the date), and it expires after 180 days, when the notice asks again. You can change your mind at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page: choosing Reject analytics cookies there stops Google Analytics, deletes its cookies for this site where the browser allows it, and a page reload unloads Google's script. Without JavaScript the notice does not appear, and Google Analytics never runs. You can also stop Google Analytics collecting data about you with Google's opt-out browser add-on. More about how Google uses data from sites that use its services is in Google's privacy information.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service from Cloudflare, our hosting provider. It counts page views, referrers, countries and browser types. It is set up without cookies and without tracking people across sites. The data is processed by Cloudflare under its own privacy statement. Its script is added by Cloudflare when the page is served, not by us.

The early-access form

The form asks for your email address and answers to five short questions, all required, plus one optional free-text answer. We use the answers only to choose early-access teams and labs and to write to you about early access. We do not sell them, share them for marketing, or use them for advertising. Please do not put patient data, personal data about other people, or confidential research details in the free-text fields.

  • Lawful basis: your consent, given by sending the form. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Who sees the answers: the Jubilab team, and Cloudflare as our hosting, storage and email provider. Each new request is also emailed to the Jubilab team as a notification.
  • How long we keep them: until early access decisions are made and for up to six months afterwards, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever is sooner.

The form stores exactly the fields in the table above. It does not store your IP address or browser details.

If you send the form again with the same email address, we keep your first answers and ignore the repeat. To change your answers, email us at the address below.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can object to or withdraw consent for our use of it. Email the address below. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
