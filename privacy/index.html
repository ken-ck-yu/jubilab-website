Privacy

Draft for the owner's review. Not legal advice. Not yet approved for publication.

This page describes what the Jubilab website does with information about you. The short version: it does very little.

Browsing this site

The site is static pages served from GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers, and loads no fonts, scripts or images from other companies. The only thing it stores in your browser is your light or dark theme choice (local storage key jubilab-theme), which never leaves your device. Like any web host, the hosting provider (GitHub) receives your IP address and basic request details when your browser fetches a page, and handles them under its own privacy statement.

The early-access form

When the form is switched on, it will ask for your email address (required) and, optionally, your role, your field, the tools you use, whether your work involves human data, and one short free-text answer about what you want to trace. We use the answers only to choose early-access labs and to write to you about early access. We do not sell them, share them for marketing, or use them for advertising. Please do not put patient data, personal data about other people, or confidential research details in the free-text field.

Until the form is switched on, nothing you type into it is sent or stored.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can object to or withdraw consent for our use of it. Email the address below. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

The Jubilab product

This page covers the website only. The Jubilab software runs on your own machine or server and, as designed, makes no outbound network calls. Its data stays with you. Any terms for the software itself come with early access.
